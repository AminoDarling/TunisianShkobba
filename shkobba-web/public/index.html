How to play

40-card deck (a standard deck without the 8, 9 and 10): Ace to 7, plus Jack (8), Queen (9) and King (10). Suits: ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣. Diamonds are the “coins”.

Pick a card from your hand. If a table card has the same value, you must take it. Otherwise you must take a set of table cards that add up to your card’s value. If nothing fits, drop the card on the table.

Clearing the whole table is a shkobba (+1), except on the last play of a deal. After a round: most cards +1, most diamonds +1, the 7 of diamonds (Sbiaa) +1.

2 players: first to 11. 4 players: you and the player opposite (your partner) are a team against the other two. Captures and points are shared, and the first team to 7 wins.

Wi‑Fi play: one person hosts (in the Android app or with the web server) and picks 2 or 4 players. Friends on the same Wi‑Fi (or the host’s hotspot) open the address shown on the host’s phone in any browser and join.
